# Concurrent Systems – Course Portfolio

**Computer Science and Engineering – University of Bologna (UniBO)**
**Academic Year 2026/2027**

---

## 📘 Overview

This repository contains the practical part of the **Concurrent Systems** course,
part of the *Master's Degree in Computer Science and Engineering* at the
**University of Bologna (UniBO)**.

The practical part consists of three assignments, one for each main part of the
course. They are not partial exams and are not graded individually: they are the
material used to structure the oral discussion, and together they form the
portfolio of what we built during the course.
